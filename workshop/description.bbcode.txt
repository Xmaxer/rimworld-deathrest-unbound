[h1]Deathrest Unbound[/h1]

Removes the deathrest building cap and uses power as the limit instead.

A deathresting pawn can use every deathrest building in the room. Each building multiplies the casket's power draw by 1.5. Deathrest buildings can also serve several caskets at once, so one accelerator can speed up every sanguophage in the room.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What changes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]No deathrest capacity.[/b] A pawn deathresting in a casket uses every deathrest building linked to it. This works for every xenotype with the deathrest gene, modded ones included.
[*][b]Exponential casket power.[/b] While someone deathrests in it, a casket draws its base power x 1.5^n, where n is the number of buildings it uses. A vanilla casket draws about 760 W with 5 buildings, 2.6 kW with 8 and 13 kW with 12.
[*][b]Shared buildings.[/b] Deathrest buildings link to every casket in their room. A building that serves several deathresting pawns at once draws its power and burns its hemogen once per casket.
[*][b]No more serum.[/b] Traders, quest rewards and loot no longer offer the deathrest capacity serum. Serum you already have still works.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Managing links[/h2]

Every casket and deathrest building gets a [b]Deathrest[/b] tab.

[list]
[*]A casket lists every building linked to it. A building lists every casket it can serve.
[*]Switch any link on or off.
[*]Each link shows its status, how long the building has worked during the current deathrest, and its power cost.
[*]On a casket, each link shows how much the total power draw changes if you toggle it.
[*]Click a name to jump to that building or casket.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Settings[/h2]

You can change all of these in the mod settings. Most changes apply immediately.

[list]
[*]Unlimited capacity, or each pawn's vanilla capacity per casket.
[*]Per-building stack limits, off by default, with an editable limit for every deathrest building type, modded ones included.
[*]The power exponent, from 1.0 to 3.0 with a default of 1.5, or no exponential power at all.
[*]Whether building power and hemogen use scale with the number of caskets served.
[*]Whether to remove the serum from traders and loot. This one needs a restart.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*]Requires Biotech and Harmony.
[*]Works with modded deathrest buildings and caskets.
[*]Also supports Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Buildings already bound to a pawn become casket links when the save loads.
[*]Safe to remove. Buildings go back to binding the vanilla way.
[/list]
