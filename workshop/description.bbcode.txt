[h1]Deathrest Unbound[/h1]

Removes the deathrest building cap. Power is the limit instead.

A deathresting pawn can use every deathrest building you give them, but each extra building makes their casket draw more power: [b]base x 1.5 per building[/b]. A few buildings are cheap. A dozen needs a power plant. Deathrest buildings can also serve several caskets at once, so one accelerator can speed up a whole room of sleeping sanguophages, as long as you can power it.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]What changes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]No deathrest capacity.[/b] A pawn deathresting in a casket uses every deathrest building linked to it. This works for every xenotype with the deathrest gene, modded ones included.
[*][b]Exponential casket power.[/b] While someone deathrests in it, a casket draws its base power x 1.5^n, where n is the number of buildings it uses. A vanilla casket draws about 760 W with 5 buildings, 2.6 kW with 8 and 13 kW with 12.
[*][b]Shared buildings.[/b] Deathrest buildings link to every casket in their room. A building serving several deathresting pawns at once draws its power, and burns its hemogen, once per casket it serves.
[*][b]No more serum.[/b] The deathrest capacity serum has nothing left to do, so traders, quest rewards and loot no longer offer it. Serum you already have still works.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Managing links[/h2]

Every casket and deathrest building gets a [b]Deathrest[/b] tab:

[list]
[*]A casket lists every building linked to it. A building lists every casket it can serve.
[*]Switch any link on or off. Links you switch off stay off.
[*]See each link's status (in use, ready, casket unpowered, out of reach), how long the building has been working for the current deathrest, and how much power it costs.
[*]On a casket, see exactly how much total power would change if you toggled each link.
[*]Click a name to jump to that building or casket.
[/list]

If a casket loses power, none of its buildings work and none of their bonuses apply, so plan your power plant before adding that tenth hemopump.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Settings[/h2]

Everything is configurable in the mod settings, and most changes apply immediately:

[list]
[*]Unlimited capacity, or the pawn's normal vanilla capacity per casket.
[*]Per-building stack limits (off by default), with an editable limit for every deathrest building type, modded ones included.
[*]The power exponent (1.0 to 3.0, default 1.5), or no exponential power at all.
[*]Whether building power and hemogen use scale with the number of caskets served.
[*]Whether to remove the serum from traders and loot (needs a restart).
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*]Requires [b]Biotech[/b] and [b]Harmony[/b].
[*]Works with modded deathrest buildings and caskets.
[*]Also supports Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage.
[*][b]Safe to add to an existing save.[/b] Buildings already bound to a pawn are converted to casket links when the save loads.
[*]Safe to remove: buildings go back to binding the vanilla way. Saves keep loading with or without the serum setting.
[/list]
